Privacy Policy

Circles App  ·  Last updated: April 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Circles ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Circles mobile application ("the App").

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, you can contact us at hello.circles.app@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and date of birth (used to verify that you are of the minimum required age).
  • Profile photo: a photo you upload to represent your profile. A profile photo is required to join or create activities.
  • Location: we may access your device location while you use the App in order to show nearby activities and improve location-based features. We do not store a continuous location history, and your precise device location is not publicly shared with other users.
  • User-generated content: activities you create or join, and messages sent through in-app support.
  • Device information: device model, operating system version, and a device identifier, used for support and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage data: in-app interactions (screens viewed, buttons tapped, funnel steps) collected via PostHog analytics to help us improve the App.
  • Subscription and purchase status: whether you have an active premium subscription. Payment card details are processed directly by Apple or Google and are never seen or stored by us.

2. Information Visible to Other Users

Certain information is visible to other users of the App in order for Circles to function as a social activity platform. This may include your first name, profile photo, approximate location, and activities you create or join. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in your profile or activity descriptions.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To show you relevant activities near your location.
  • To process subscriptions and one-time purchases.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g. password reset).
  • To provide customer support.
  • To analyse usage patterns and improve the App's features and performance.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on your location, we process personal data under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide and operate the App.
  • Legitimate interests — to improve the App, prevent abuse, maintain security, and analyse usage.
  • Compliance with legal obligations — where processing is required by law.
  • Consent — where required by applicable law.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google / Firebase — authentication and cloud infrastructure. Privacy policy
  • PostHog — product analytics. Privacy policy
  • Mapbox — maps and location search. Privacy policy
  • RevenueCat — subscription management. Privacy policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing. Apple · Google
  • Cloudflare R2 — file storage (e.g. profile photos). Privacy policy

6. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers used by the App may process or store data outside your country of residence. Where required by applicable law, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. Users may request account deletion through the App or by contacting us. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello.circles.app@gmail.com.

If you are located in the UK, you may also lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

9. User Safety

Users are responsible for exercising appropriate caution and judgement when interacting with other users or attending activities organised through the App.

10. Age Requirement

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. Because Circles facilitates in-person meetings and direct messaging between strangers, we require all users to confirm they are at least 18 years old during onboarding. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will terminate their account and delete their data.

11. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may notify users through the App or by email, and will update the "Last updated" date above.
